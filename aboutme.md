## About me

I'm currently a math master student at Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile under the supervision of [Eduardo Cerpa](https://www.mat.uc.cl/~eduardo.cerpa/). I did my mathematical engineering studies at Universidad Técnica Federico Santa María under the supervision of [Patricio Guzmán](http://pguzman.mat.utfsm.cl/) and [Hugo Parada](https://sites.google.com/view/hugo-parada).

## Research interests

I'm interested in Control and Stabilization of Partial Differential Equations. Also I've been learning tools from microlocal analysis.
